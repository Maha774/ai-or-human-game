# 🎃 AI or Human?

A spooky guessing game. You read 10 short texts and guess whether a human or an AI wrote each one. After every answer you see the giveaway, so you learn how AI writing sounds.

Play it here:https://coruscating-licorice-f35202.netlify.app

## What's inside
- 10 texts about older people and college life (funny, real and sad)
- Instant feedback with a reason after every answer
- Random question order every time
- Score screen with a "copy my score" button
- Red and black Halloween theme

## Built with
HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No libraries, no backend.

## What I learned
AI loves words like "journey", "navigate" and "transformative". Human writing is messier and more specific.

## Run it yourself
Download the files and open `index.html` in your browser.
